Hullo! My name is Carnun1, and this is my little corner of the web.

I have more interests than I know to do with. Music and physics captivated me as a teen. Then came philosophy2 and computers. I like writing, but I don't do it nearly enough. (YET.) Since putting School behind me I have spent most of my time solving problems with Clojure3, at startups. I feel very lucky that I get to say that.

In what time is spare, I am currently working on two projects:

  1. carnun.com, this site. (Well, what it will become: a choose-your-own-adventure through a fractal garden of deeply interlinked essays and bl*gposts.)
  2. watt.do, an app for principled fun-following.

And when I don't feel like anything so serious, I tinker with a small grid-based puzzle game, currently called SNEKS, about what happens when Snake-like snakes swallow awkwardly-shaped food and must fit through gaps and past each other.
